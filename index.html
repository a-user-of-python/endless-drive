ENDLESS DRIVE

    
Soft-body edition. Every car is a cage of nodes and beams — hit something and the
      front crumples, beams bend and snap, and the back of the car keeps coming until its beams stop it.
      Endless twisting road, and cliffs you cannot climb back up.

BEAM RESOLUTION

      
Per-car beam counts are set in game with [ and ] and remembered per car.

    

    

      W A S D / arrowsdrive (S brakes, then reverses at a stop)
      Spacehandbrake
      Ccamera: chase / cockpit / freecam
      Bshow the node & beam skeleton (green = good, orange = bent, red = broken)
      Rreset — fresh car on the road
      Frepair in place — only at a full stop
      1 – 5switch car
      [ / ]beam count of the current car — Normal 150 / High 250 / Ultra 500 nodes, then +10 nodes per press up to 1000 (respawns the car; each car remembers its own setting)
      Tbeam display on / off
      P / Escpause
